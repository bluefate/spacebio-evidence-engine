# One-Minute Video Plan

This cut uses the four generated clips in `docs/final/videos/` plus the existing project screenshots.

## Goal

Create a tight, polished 60-second demo that communicates:

- space biology evidence is fragmented
- the app keeps evidence grounded in a controlled corpus
- search, ask, and citations stay inspectable
- the system fails closed instead of guessing

## Fast edit notes

- Keep all generated clips short and cinematic.
- Use hard cuts or simple cross-dissolves only.
- Add a slow zoom on screenshots in CapCut.
- Keep background music very low under narration.
- Avoid extra AI visuals unless you have spare time.

## Suggested audio

- Calm documentary-style voice
- Medium-slow pace
- Low ambient music
- No dramatic sound effects

## If you need a single-line hook

Use this as the opening title card:

`Space Biology Evidence Engine`

Subtitle:

`Citation-first research from a controlled corpus`
